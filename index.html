FEATURED EXPERIENCE5 min read

Our experience at Skill Power Institute was meaningful, exciting, and educational. As students taking NC II training, we learned different skills related to housekeeping and hospitality. Through our hands-on activities, we gained knowledge and experience that helped us understand the importance of cleanliness, discipline, teamwork, and professionalism.

01

Bed-Making Training

Bed making is an important housekeeping skill because a neatly prepared bed helps create a clean, comfortable, and welcoming room. During training, we learned the value of arranging bedding carefully, keeping the bed presentable, and paying attention to small details. The activity showed us that proper preparation, neatness, and consistency are important in maintaining hospitality standards.

02

Using the Vacuum Cleaner and Polisher

We also learned about housekeeping equipment, including the vacuum cleaner and floor polisher. These tools help maintain clean floors and a tidy environment. Learning about their proper use reminded us to handle equipment responsibly, follow safety precautions, and keep the work area organized. Choosing and using the right equipment is part of providing efficient and professional housekeeping service.

03

Learning the Duties of a Butler

Our NC II experience also introduced us to the role of a butler in hospitality. Butler service requires courtesy, attentiveness, respect, and professionalism. A butler helps meet guests’ needs while maintaining a high standard of service and privacy. This lesson helped us appreciate that hospitality is not only about completing tasks; it is also about making people feel comfortable and respected.

04

Hands-On Practice and Teamwork

Hands-on activities gave us the opportunity to connect what we learned with practical skills. Training also highlighted the importance of teamwork, communication, patience, and following instructions. Working toward a shared goal helps tasks become more organized and effective. These qualities are useful in training and in any future workplace, especially in the hospitality industry.

05

What We Learned from Our Experience

Our experience taught us that cleanliness, discipline, attention to detail, and professionalism are essential in housekeeping and hospitality. We learned that practical skills improve with guidance, preparation, and continued practice. We also gained a better understanding of the responsibility involved in caring for spaces and serving guests. These lessons can guide us as we continue learning and preparing for future opportunities.

LOOKING AHEAD

Conclusion

Overall, our training at Skill Power Institute was a valuable learning experience. It helped us develop practical knowledge and understand the standards expected in housekeeping and hospitality. The skills and values we gained—cleanliness, discipline, teamwork, and professionalism—will help prepare us for future work in the hospitality industry.

END OF STORYThank you for reading.
